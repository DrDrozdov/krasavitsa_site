# Krasavitsa product context

## Product

Krasavitsa is a mobile-first beauty AI service for women aged 20–40. It turns a natural-language request, budget, routine, sensitivities, and taste into an explained selection for skincare, haircare, or fragrance. The website, profile, editorial content, and Telegram bot are one product, not separate campaigns.

## Positioning

Krasavitsa is a calm beauty workspace that reduces the noise of cosmetic choice. It is not a marketplace, a medical service, or a generic purple AI assistant. The product should feel like an independent beauty editor with the memory and speed of a personal digital tool.

## Primary user journey

1. Understand the promise in one mobile viewport.
2. Choose skin, hair, or fragrance without reading a long explanation.
3. Describe the real situation in normal language.
4. See a purposeful search state with no dead time.
5. Receive a compact, explained result and save it to a profile.
6. Return through history, favorites, editorial content, or Telegram.

## Conversion goals

- Primary: start an AI selection.
- Secondary: create or open a profile and save the result.
- Supporting: continue in Telegram, read editorial material, follow the project.

## Brand belief ladder

- Krasavitsa understands the context, not just keywords.
- Every recommendation explains why it belongs in the scenario.
- Budget, sensitivities, routine, and preferences are respected.
- The profile remembers useful context and does not expose search text as identity data.
- The same logic works across the website and Telegram bot.

## Brand personality

Tactile, self-assured, intelligent, caring, and editorial. The interface should create calm curiosity first and informed confidence second. It may be bold, but never loud or patronizing.

## Visual direction

- Deep bordeaux and raspberry provide identity and action.
- Powder, warm ivory, and restrained violet provide softness.
- Light and dark themes have equal status and verified contrast.
- Natural skin texture and sculptural beauty-lab photography replace generic stock imagery.
- One expressive motion object carries each important interaction. Decorative motion must not compete with it.
- Mobile surfaces are compact, flat, and image-led. Avoid nested glass cards, oversized pills, and empty scroll length.

## Anti-references

- Generic female e-commerce storefronts.
- Purple-gradient AI SaaS landing pages.
- Oversized translucent cards with low-contrast text.
- A permanent logo-and-name strip that consumes the first mobile row.
- Decorative blocks that repeat the same promise without adding proof or action.

## Content principles

- Start with the user situation, not product technology.
- Use concise Russian with direct verbs and no exaggerated claims.
- Prefer an explanation, a concrete next step, or proof over promotional filler.
- Do not present cosmetic guidance as medical diagnosis.

## Accessibility and quality bar

- WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
- Minimum 44 by 44 pixel touch targets.
- Keyboard-operable navigation, dialogs, forms, and theme controls.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks.
- Theme is applied before paint and stays synchronized with system settings until the user chooses a manual override.
- Mobile layouts must remain useful at 320 CSS pixels and avoid horizontal scrolling.
